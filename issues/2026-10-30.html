With daylight saving time ending this Sunday and darker evenings arriving, it's a good moment to think about seasonal mood and energy: getting outside during daylight hours, even briefly, can meaningfully help with the mood dip many people feel as the days shorten — experts generally recommend aiming for a short walk in natural light each day. As always, talk to your doctor if you have personal health concerns.

Halloween candy season is in full swing — a gentle reminder that a few simple habits go a long way for kids' dental health: rinsing with water after sweets, brushing before bed, and spacing out treats rather than grazing all day can make a real difference. Your child's dentist is always the best resource for personalized advice.

Money-Saving Tips for Families

With Halloween behind us, many stores will begin marking down fall and Halloween merchandise by 50% or more starting this weekend — it's a great time to stock up on costumes, decorations, and candy for next year at a fraction of the regular price.

The end of October is a smart time to check your FSA (Flexible Spending Account) balance if your employer offers one — many FSA plans have a year-end use-it-or-lose-it deadline. Common eligible expenses include kids' prescriptions, vision care, dental visits, and some over-the-counter items. Check your plan documents or ask your HR department for your specific deadline and rules.

Buy Nothing groups and local Facebook swap groups are a fantastic way to score free or low-cost kids' Halloween costumes, winter coats, and cold-weather gear right now — neighbors are often clearing out last year's sizes just as your kids need them. Search for Strongsville Buy Nothing groups on Facebook to get started.

The Cuyahoga County Public Library's museum pass lending program lets Strongsville cardholders borrow free or discounted passes to area attractions — including the Cleveland Museum of Natural History and Cleveland Metroparks Zoo. A great way to plan a family outing this fall without the full admission cost. Check availability at the library's website.

Weekend Ideas for Young Families

🏞️ Head out to Boulder Farm Park or Poppy Hill Park this Halloween weekend for a crisp fall walk — the leaf color is at or near its peak and the trails are beautiful right now. Pack a thermos of hot cider and let the kids crunch through the leaves before the evening trick-or-treating fun begins.
🏞️ Check out the Cuyahoga County Public Library Strongsville branch's upcoming programs for toddlers and school-age kids — from Toddler Sensory and Art Play to Music and Movement sessions, the library has a warm, free, and engaging option for families looking for something low-key this weekend as the weather cools down.
🏞️ If you're looking for a low-cost family outing Saturday or Sunday, Mill Stream Run Reservation in the Cleveland Metroparks (just minutes from Strongsville) offers gorgeous fall trail walks, a disc golf course, and a nature center that is especially fun for curious kids — a great way to burn off Halloween candy energy in the fresh air.
