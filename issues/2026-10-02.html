Health & Wellness

Early October is peak flu season preparation time — the CDC recommends getting your flu shot by the end of October for the best protection, so now is a great moment to schedule one for everyone in the family, including children 6 months and older. Check with your pediatrician, local pharmacy, or the Cuyahoga County Board of Health for convenient options near Strongsville. As always, consult your own doctor with any personal health questions.

As the days grow shorter heading into fall, it's worth paying attention to mood and energy levels — seasonal shifts in daylight can affect sleep, motivation, and overall wellbeing for both kids and adults. Getting outside for even a short walk during daylight hours, keeping consistent sleep schedules, and staying connected with friends and neighbors can make a real difference. If you or someone in your family is struggling, reaching out to a healthcare provider is always a good first step.

Cool fall weather means more layers — and more opportunities for kids to skip the basics like handwashing after outdoor play. Back-to-school season brings a rise in cold and stomach bug transmission, so reinforcing simple hygiene habits (wash hands before meals, after blowing your nose, and after being in public spaces) is one of the most effective things families can do to stay healthy this fall. Check with your family doctor for personalized prevention advice.

Money-Saving Tips for Families

Fall is a great time to stock up on next year's summer clothing for your kids at deep clearance prices — many retailers mark down shorts, swimwear, and lightweight tops by 50–70% in September and October. Buy a size or two up and tuck it away for next year.

If you have a Flexible Spending Account (FSA) for dependent care or healthcare, check your balance now — many FSA plans have a use-it-or-lose-it deadline at year-end (December 31), and October is the right time to schedule any eligible appointments or purchase eligible items so you don't forfeit funds. Check your plan documents or HR department for your specific deadline and eligible expenses.

The Cuyahoga County Public Library lends museum passes that give free or discounted admission to top Greater Cleveland attractions — including the Cleveland Museum of Natural History, the Cleveland Museum of Art, the Children's Museum of Cleveland, and more. Borrowing a pass instead of paying full admission can save a family of four $40 or more on a single outing.

For gently used kids' clothing and gear, the Buy Nothing groups on Facebook (search for your Strongsville-area neighborhood group) are a fantastic free resource — neighbors regularly post outgrown Halloween costumes, winter coats, and kids' toys, especially in October as families prep for the season. It's free, local, and keeps good stuff out of the landfill.

Weekend Ideas for Young Families

🏞️ Head over to Cleveland Metroparks Mill Stream Run Reservation this weekend for a fall foliage hike — the trails are beautiful in early October and the nature center is a great stop if you have curious little ones who want to learn what lives in the woods. The reservation is just minutes from Strongsville and parking is free.
🏞️ Visit Boulder Farm Park or Poppy Hill Park for an old-fashioned fall afternoon outside — pack a picnic lunch, bring a frisbee, and enjoy the crispness in the air before the leaves really start to drop. These lesser-visited Strongsville parks tend to be quieter than the big playgrounds, which makes them perfect for toddlers and younger kids who need room to roam.
🏞️ Check out the Cuyahoga County Public Library's Strongsville branch this Saturday for their seasonal programs — the library's maker space and craft sessions are especially fun this time of year, and they also lend museum passes if you want to plan a bigger outing to a Cleveland museum next week for free or reduced admission. Check the CCPL website for the current schedule before you go.
