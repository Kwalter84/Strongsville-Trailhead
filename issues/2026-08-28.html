Health & Wellness

Southwest General is offering a free community health talk on stroke awareness and prevention - a great opportunity for Strongsville-area residents to learn the warning signs and risk factors directly from local health professionals. Check the City of Strongsville website for dates and registration details. As always, talk to your own doctor about your personal health questions.

Read more →

With kids back in school and the season shifting, now is a good time to think about back-to-school health basics: establish consistent sleep routines (most school-age kids need 9-11 hours), encourage regular handwashing especially after school, and schedule any overdue well-child checkups before the fall rush. Consult your family's doctor for personalized guidance.

Late August heat can linger well into the school year - if your kids are playing fall sports outdoors, watch for signs of heat exhaustion during warm afternoon practices: excessive sweating, dizziness, or nausea. Make sure they're drinking water before, during, and after activity, and not just waiting until they feel thirsty. Always follow your doctor's advice for specific health concerns.

Money-Saving Tips for Families

Ohio periodically runs a back-to-school sales-tax holiday in late summer that can cover clothing, school supplies, and certain other items - the exact dates, dollar thresholds, and eligible item categories change year to year, so check Ohio's official tax department website or a trusted news source for the current-year specifics rather than relying on last year's rules.

Read more →

The Cuyahoga County Public Library's museum-pass lending program lets cardholders borrow free or discounted admission passes to local attractions including the Cleveland Museum of Natural History, Great Lakes Science Center, and the Zoo - a fantastic way to do a Labor Day weekend outing without paying full price. Check the library's website to see what's available and reserve ahead.

Read more →

As kids outgrow back-to-school clothes faster than you can blink, consider shopping end-of-summer clearance racks now and buying one size up for next year - retailers typically mark down summer clothing significantly in late August and early September, so you can stock up on shorts, swimwear, and warm-weather basics for next year at a fraction of the price.

Local Buy Nothing groups and Facebook Marketplace are especially active this time of year as families do back-to-school cleanouts - it's an ideal moment to post outgrown kids' gear, sports equipment, and clothing, and to find what your own kids need for the new school year at no cost or low cost from neighbors right here in Strongsville.

Weekend Ideas for Young Families

🏞️ Head out to Cleveland Metroparks Mill Stream Run Reservation this weekend for a late-August nature walk - the trails along the Rocky River are beautifully shaded and the late-summer wildflowers are at their peak. Kids can look for frogs and turtles along the riverbank, and the wide paved paths are great for younger children in strollers. It's a free, easy outing that feels like a real escape without leaving the area.
🏞️ Visit the Strongsville branch of the Cuyahoga County Public Library and ask about borrowing a museum pass - the library lends free or reduced-admission passes to local attractions including the Cleveland Museum of Natural History and the Cleveland Metroparks Zoo, making a Labor Day weekend museum trip very affordable. It's also a great chance to browse the new fall reading displays and pick up a few books before summer officially winds down.
🏞️ Catch an end-of-summer treat at one of Strongsville's locally-owned ice cream or frozen custard spots this weekend - late August is prime season, and weekend evenings are perfect for an after-dinner cone outing with the family before the weather starts to cool. Make it a little event by letting each kid pick their own flavor combination, and enjoy a few more of those warm summer nights while they last.
