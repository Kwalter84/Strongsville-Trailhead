Health & Wellness

Fall is peak flu season — the CDC recommends everyone 6 months and older get their annual flu shot by the end of October for the best protection. Most local pharmacies and pediatrician offices offer them now, often with no appointment needed. Check with your own doctor about what's right for your family.

As the days get shorter and temperatures drop, it's a good time to talk with your kids about the importance of sleep routines — kids ages 6-12 need 9-12 hours per night, and consistent bedtimes help their immune systems stay strong heading into cold and flu season. Always consult your child's pediatrician with specific concerns.

October can quietly bring on seasonal mood changes as daylight shrinks — if you or someone in your household is feeling more down or sluggish than usual, getting outside during daylight hours, even for a short walk, can make a real difference. If symptoms persist, reach out to your family doctor.

Money-Saving Tips for Families

October is a smart time to stock up on kids' Halloween costumes and fall clothing at end-of-season clearance prices — many retailers start discounting seasonal items mid-month, and buying a size up means next year's costume is already handled.

The Cuyahoga County Public Library's museum pass program lets cardholders borrow free or reduced-price passes to area museums and attractions — a great way to do something memorable with the family without paying full admission. Check their website for currently available passes.

Read more →

If your kids have outgrown fall and winter gear from last year, now is a great time to list it on Buy Nothing groups or local Facebook swap groups before buying new — and to score next year's sizes from other families doing the same thing. ThredUp Kids and Kidizen are also worth browsing for gently used coats, boots, and layers at a fraction of retail.

Read more →

Year-end FSA (Flexible Spending Account) deadlines are approaching for many families — if you have an FSA through your employer, check your remaining balance now. Eligible expenses include copays, glasses, dental work, and many over-the-counter health items. Use it or lose it rules vary by plan, so check with your HR department soon.

Weekend Ideas for Young Families

🏞️ Head out to Cleveland Metroparks Mill Stream Run Reservation this weekend for some beautiful fall color on the trails — the leaves are at or near peak in the Strongsville area right now, and the disc golf course is a fun addition if you have older kids or teens in tow. Pack a picnic and make an afternoon of it.
🏞️ Stop by the Cuyahoga County Public Library Strongsville Branch and check out their museum pass lending program — you can borrow free or discounted passes to places like the Cleveland Museum of Natural History or the Great Lakes Science Center, making a rainy fall weekend outing much easier on the wallet. Ask at the circulation desk for what's currently available.
🏞️ Visit one of the area's fall farm stands or pick-your-own pumpkin patches this weekend while the season is still going strong — Duck Pond Park is a lovely spot to walk afterward and let little ones enjoy the fall scenery close to home before heading back with your pumpkin haul.
