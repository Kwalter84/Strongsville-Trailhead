As summer winds down and kids head back to school, early September is the ideal time to schedule flu shots for the whole family - most pharmacies and pediatricians begin stocking vaccines now, and getting vaccinated in early fall gives your immune system the best head start before flu season peaks. As always, check with your family's doctor about the best timing for your household.

With outdoor sports and activities ramping up this fall, it's a good moment to revisit tick safety - Cuyahoga County's wooded trails and parks are beautiful this time of year, but ticks remain active well into autumn. Do a quick tick check after any outdoor time, and consult your doctor if you notice a rash or unexplained fever after a hike.

Money-Saving Tips for Families

Early September is prime back-to-school shopping season, and many national retailers run their largest clothing clearance events of the year right now as summer inventory clears out. It's also a great time to buy next summer's clothes in a size up for growing kids at steep end-of-season discounts - check the clearance racks at stores near SouthPark Mall this weekend.

Ohio occasionally runs a sales-tax holiday on school supplies and clothing in late summer or early fall - rules and eligible items change year to year, so check the Ohio Department of Taxation's website for the current year's exact dates, thresholds, and qualifying items rather than relying on last year's details.

Weekend Ideas for Young Families

🏞️ Take advantage of the last warm weekends of the season with a family outing to Cleveland Metroparks Mill Stream Run Reservation in Strongsville - the trails wind through beautiful Chippewa Creek gorge, and early September is a lovely time to spot the first subtle hints of fall color starting to appear on the trees. Pack a picnic and let the kids explore the natural play areas near the creek.
🏞️ Check out the Cuyahoga County Public Library Strongsville Branch's fall programming lineup - September often brings new storytimes, STEAM workshops, and maker-space sessions that are perfect for school-age kids. The library also lends museum passes that can get your family into local attractions for free or reduced admission, so it's worth asking at the desk what's currently available.
🏞️ Head over to Boulder Farm Park or Poppy Hill Park for some unstructured outdoor play this weekend - these neighborhood parks are quieter alternatives to the bigger trailheads and are great for younger kids who just want to run around, use the playground equipment, and enjoy the early-fall air before the weather shifts. Bring sidewalk chalk or a frisbee to make an afternoon of it.
