Health & Wellness

Southwest General is hosting a free Health Talk on Healthy Eating During the Holidays — a timely and practical program as the season of big meals and sweet treats gets underway; check with the City of Strongsville for date and registration details.

Read more →

As the days get shorter and temperatures drop, this is a great time to talk to your doctor about your annual flu shot if you haven't had one yet — the CDC generally recommends getting vaccinated by the end of October for best protection heading into peak flu season. As always, check with your own healthcare provider for personalized advice.

Late October is also a good moment to check in on your family's sleep routines — kids' schedules often drift as fall activities pile up, and consistent sleep is one of the best defenses against seasonal colds and bugs. Aim for age-appropriate bedtimes and wind-down routines, and consult your child's pediatrician with any specific questions.

Money-Saving Tips for Families

With Halloween just around the corner and Thanksgiving and the winter holidays following close behind, this is a smart time to think ahead: many retailers launch early holiday sales and doorbuster deals in late October and early November, often before Black Friday itself. Browsing store apps and signing up for email lists now can help you snag deals before inventory runs low on popular kids' items.

If your kids have outgrown their costumes, snow gear, or fall clothing, the Buy Nothing Strongsville groups on Facebook are a great place to find free kids' items from neighbors — and to pass along what your own children have grown out of. It's one of the best kept community secrets for stretching the family budget.

Don't overlook the Cuyahoga County Public Library's museum pass lending program — cardholders can borrow free or discounted passes to area museums, nature centers, and attractions. It's a fantastic way to plan affordable family day trips this fall without paying full admission prices.

Read more →

For families approaching year-end, now is a good time to check whether your employer offers a Flexible Spending Account (FSA) or Dependent Care FSA with a use-it-or-lose-it deadline — many plans require funds to be spent by December 31st. Eligible expenses can include copays, glasses, dental visits, and some dependent care costs. Check your plan documents or HR for your specific deadlines and rules.
