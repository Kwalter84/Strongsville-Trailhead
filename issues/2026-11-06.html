Health & Wellness

With daylight saving time now in the rearview mirror and shorter days here, it's a great time to check in on your family's sleep routines - kids especially tend to need a consistent bedtime even when it gets dark earlier, and the extra darkness can affect everyone's mood heading into winter.

November is a prime time to make sure the whole family is up to date on flu shots if you haven't gotten them yet - most pediatricians, pharmacies, and urgent care clinics offer them with little or no wait; check with your own doctor or healthcare provider to find out what's right for your family.

Cold and dry air can wreak havoc on kids' skin and sinuses as the heat kicks on indoors - consider running a humidifier in bedrooms overnight and remind kids to wash hands frequently, especially coming home from school, to cut down on the spread of seasonal colds. As always, talk to your family's doctor for personalized health guidance.

Money-Saving Tips for Families

With the holiday shopping season officially upon us, now is a smart time to map out your gift list and set a firm budget before Black Friday and Cyber Monday deals start arriving in your inbox. Buying with intention rather than reacting to flash sales is one of the best ways to avoid overspending - decide what you're buying before the sale, not because of it.

If you have a Flexible Spending Account (FSA) or Dependent Care FSA through your employer, check your remaining balance now - many FSA funds must be used by December 31st or you forfeit them. Eligible expenses can include copays, glasses, contacts, and certain kids' health items; check your plan documents or ask your HR department for the specifics.

The Cuyahoga County Public Library's museum pass lending program lets cardholders borrow free or discounted passes to area attractions including local museums and cultural sites - a great way to plan affordable family day trips this fall and winter without paying full admission. Check availability at the Strongsville branch or online.

Read more →

Buying kids' winter gear a size up at end-of-season clearance sales (typically January through March) can save your family significant money compared to buying current-size gear at full price right now - but if you need something this season, check apps like ThredUp Kids or local Buy Nothing groups first before paying retail.

Weekend Ideas for Young Families

🏞️ Head out to Cleveland Metroparks Mill Stream Run Reservation this weekend for a crisp November hike through the fall foliage - the trails are especially beautiful this time of year with the leaves down and the creek running, and the wide paved path is easy for strollers and younger kids. Dress in layers and bring a warm thermos; it's a free, unhurried way to get outside before the really cold weather sets in.
🏞️ Check out the Strongsville branch of Cuyahoga County Public Library for their current fall programs - the library offers storytimes broken out by age group on different days of the week, and the indoor cozy atmosphere is a perfect contrast to the November chill. It's also a great time to ask about the library's museum-pass lending program if you want to plan a bigger family outing in the coming weeks.
🏞️ Take the kids over to Poppy Hill Park or Boulder Farm Park for some unstructured outdoor play while the ground is still dry - these neighborhood parks are quieter than the busier destinations and give little ones a chance to run, climb, and explore at their own pace on a cool Saturday morning before settling in for a warm lunch at home.
