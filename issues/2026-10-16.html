Money-Saving Tips for Families

The Cuyahoga County Public Library's museum pass lending program lets cardholders borrow free or discounted passes to area attractions including science centers and art museums — a genuinely unbeatable deal for family outings this fall.

Read more →

Mid-October is a smart time to start holiday shopping early — popular toys and gifts tend to sell out or spike in price as November approaches. Buying even a few gifts now at regular price beats paying a premium (or missing out entirely) closer to the holidays.

Check out Buy Nothing groups on Facebook for your local Strongsville neighborhood — this time of year people are often passing along gently used Halloween costumes, kids' cold-weather gear, and outgrown clothing for free. It's a great way to kit out the kids for the season without spending a dime.

If you have an FSA (Flexible Spending Account) or dependent care FSA through your employer, now is the time to check your remaining balance — many plans have a use-it-or-lose-it deadline at year-end. Eligible expenses can include prescription eyewear, dental work, and some over-the-counter health items for the whole family. Check with your plan administrator for your specific deadline and covered items.

Weekend Ideas for Young Families

🏞️ Head out to Cleveland Metroparks Mill Stream Run Reservation this weekend for a crisp fall hike — the leaves are at or near peak color right now, and the trails around the Chippewa Creek gorge are stunning in mid-October. Pack a thermos of hot cocoa and let the kids splash through fallen leaves on the easier loop trails. The nature center is open on weekends if you want to warm up and learn something along the way.
🏞️ Visit Boulder Farm Park or Poppy Hill Park for an afternoon of free outdoor play — both are quieter neighborhood gems that are easy to enjoy on a cool fall afternoon without the crowds. Bring a frisbee or a soccer ball and make the most of the last comfortable outdoor weekends before the real cold settles in.
🏞️ Stop by the Cuyahoga County Public Library Strongsville branch and check out their museum pass lending program — you can borrow passes to area museums (including the Cleveland Museum of Natural History and the Great Lakes Science Center) for free or reduced admission. It's a fantastic way to turn a rainy or chilly October afternoon into an affordable family adventure. Call ahead or check the CCPL website to see what passes are currently available.
