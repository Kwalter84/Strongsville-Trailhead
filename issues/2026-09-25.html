Money-Saving Tips for Families

Fall is a smart time to buy kids' winter gear a size up during early-season sales before the coldest weather hits — many retailers discount last season's coats, boots, and snow pants in September and October, and buying slightly big means your child can wear it this year and next.

The Cuyahoga County Public Library's Strongsville branch lends museum passes that give your family free or reduced admission to major Cleveland-area attractions — it's one of the best-kept budget secrets in town, and passes can be reserved online ahead of your visit.

Read more →

If you have a Flexible Spending Account (FSA) for dependent care or healthcare, check your balance now — many FSA plans have a use-it-or-lose-it deadline at year-end, and fall is a great time to schedule eligible appointments (eye exams, dental checkups, pediatric visits) before the December crunch.

Secondhand kids' clothing apps and platforms like ThredUp can be a great way to fill out your children's fall and winter wardrobes for a fraction of retail prices — sizes turn over quickly as kids grow, so the selection tends to be especially good heading into the cooler seasons.

Read more →

Weekend Ideas for Young Families

🏞️ Head out to the Strongsville Historical Village's Harvest Festival this weekend for a quintessential fall afternoon — think autumn decorations, historical demonstrations, and a relaxed pace that's perfect for kids who love hands-on learning and seasonal fun. It's one of those low-key, genuinely local events that reminds you why living here is great.
🏞️ Take the family on a fall nature walk through Cleveland Metroparks Mill Stream Run Reservation, which runs right through the Strongsville area — the leaf color is just starting to turn and the trails along the creek are especially pretty this time of year. Pack a snack, let the kids splash near the water's edge, and enjoy the cool air before the really cold weather arrives.
🏞️ Swing by the Cuyahoga County Public Library's Strongsville branch to check out the museum pass lending program if you haven't used it lately — you can borrow free or reduced-admission passes to places like the Cleveland Museum of Natural History or the Great Lakes Science Center, making a fall day trip very affordable. It's worth looking at what's available for the coming weeks while fall programming is in full swing.
